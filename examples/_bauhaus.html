Brutalist / Bauhaus study

Same character, different rules: primitives, flat fills, primaries, weight from mass rather than light.

A · Bauhaus primitives

Square, circle, triangle only. Three primaries, no tints. The eyes are different shapes rather than different colours — the asymmetry does the work a highlight used to.

B · Brutalist mass

One concrete slab, one hard offset shadow, no gradient anywhere. Depth is stated, not simulated — the shadow is a flat block with a straight edge.

C · The two combined

Brutalist mass, Bauhaus vocabulary: a heavy square, a single primary accent, and a stencil visor. This is the one that would still read at 26px.

D · Neo-brutalist

Thick black outline, hard offset shadow, flat saturated fill. The animation is a nudge: it shunts into its own shadow and back, which is the only motion this style really allows.

E · Constructivist

Diagonal axis, red and black, no horizon. The eye is a single wedge that rotates in steps — direction as the whole personality.

F · Terminal

Phosphor on black, a scanline that drifts, a block cursor that blinks on the half second. The most honest about being software.

C1 · baseline

Where we got to. Three tonal bands, stencil visor, red antenna.

C2 · one slot

A single wide aperture instead of two eyes, with one accent block that slides inside it. Reads as an instrument rather than a face — and the slide is the only motion it needs.

C3 · squat

Wider than tall, no antenna, accent as a band under the visor. The proportion alone changes the character from alert to steady.

C4 · inverted

Dark mass, light aperture. The same object at night — and the one that would sit best on a light page, since the mark stops competing with the background.

C5 · grille

A speaker grille under the visor gives it a front — a top and a bottom, so it has a way up. Three bars that light in sequence while it is working.

C6 · offset eye

One square eye, off centre. Asymmetry is what the earlier version was missing: a perfectly centred face reads as an icon, and one thing out of line reads as attention.

G · Swiss reduction

One circle, one bar, one accent, strict grid. Nothing to animate but position — so it breathes on a slow, even cycle and does nothing else.
